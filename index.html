Privacy

An account is only needed to play with others. Alone, you play without one, and your cities stay in your browser.

What the server keeps

Your username, your email address, your password (in a form that cannot be read back), the language of your emails, the sessions of the devices where you are logged in, when your account was created, changed and last used, its failed logins (to protect it), and a new email address until you confirm it. If you play with others: your shared cities and regions, the ones you are a member of, the regions whose owner removed you, and the invitations you send and receive. The chat is never stored: the server keeps the last 50 messages of a city in memory while it is open, and forgets them when it closes, at most a minute after its last player leaves.

The server's logs record the IP addresses and the accounts behind requests, to protect it against abuse. Old logs are deleted as new ones are written, by the log rotation that the people who run this server set up.

Backups of the server's data, made by the people who run it, keep what they hold until they delete them.

Why

Only to run your account and the cities you play with others: nothing is sold or shared, and there is no advertising and no tracking.

Cookies

A single cookie keeps you logged in. It is strictly necessary, so the game does not ask for your consent. Your browser also remembers your language, your view and your saved city, without sending them anywhere.

Your data

On your account's screen, you can download everything kept about your account, change your email address and password, and delete the account with all it holds.

                          Neighbouring cities

                          Make deals with the cities around yours. Most need a link reaching their edge of the map: build it, and the deal comes into force.

                          Trade agreement
                          Shops and factories find more customers: more demand for commercial and industrial zones.
                          Buy electricity
                          Their power enters your grid by the power line at their edge: as much as a gas power plant gives.
                          Sell electricity
                          Your spare power goes to them by the power line at their edge: as much as a gas power plant gives.
                          Twinning
                          Exchanges between the two cities make yours better known: more people want to move in. No link needed.

                          Deals are paid with the yearly budget, for the months they were in force. Bigger cities make bigger deals.
